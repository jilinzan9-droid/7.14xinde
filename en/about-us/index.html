Who Are We?

Jinan Prite Petroleum Equipment Co., Ltd., operating under the brand Pratt Oil, is a professional supplier of oilfield equipment and spare parts based in Jinan, China.

We specialize in replacement parts and components for oil and gas drilling, workover, well servicing, cementing, and fracturing operations. Our customers include drilling contractors, oilfield service companies, equipment distributors, repair workshops, and maintenance companies worldwide.

Our Product Range

Our main product range includes:

  • Spare parts for F-series, HHF, 3NB, NOV, EMSCO, and Gardner Denver mud pumps
  • Spare parts for SPM TWS and TPB/TPC/TPD 600 high-pressure plunger pumps
  • Drawworks, transmission, and braking system components
  • Eaton Airflex WCB auxiliary brake parts
  • PS-series hydraulic disc brake parts
  • Brake bands, pneumatic clutches, PTOs, and push-type clutches
  • Hydraulic pumps, valves, cylinders, and hydraulic components
  • Workover rig parts, wellhead tools, and high-pressure equipment components

Parts can be supplied according to the part number, technical drawing, equipment nameplate, sample, or product photo.

What We Do

Pratt Oil focuses on providing practical and reliable spare-parts solutions for the maintenance, repair, and overhaul of oilfield equipment.

We can support both regular purchasing programs and urgent replacement requirements. Whether the customer needs standard wear parts, complete repair kits, major assemblies, or hard-to-find components, our team can assist with product identification, technical confirmation, quotation, inspection, packing, and international delivery.

Why Choose Us

Over the years, Pratt Oil has established stable cooperation with experienced manufacturers and suppliers throughout China.

Our strong supply network enables us to provide reliable quality, competitive prices, flexible solutions, and efficient delivery. We are committed to building long-term partnerships and providing responsive support to customers worldwide.
