When ordering 8T-650-01 mud pump parts, start with the component drawing reference and the actual pump configuration. The two supplied Russian documents concern the same model, written 8Т-650-01; the letters РЭ identify the operating-manual document, not an additional pump model.

Check the edition before using a dimension

The supplied parts catalogue prints a 203 mm piston stroke, while the 2003 operating manual prints 205 ± 0.5 mm. This guide preserves both source values instead of choosing one without evidence. Confirm the installed pump and its original drawing before ordering stroke-dependent components.

The manual also gives different conditions for the 45 L/s flow figure and the 32 MPa discharge-pressure figure. They use different piston diameters. These figures must not be combined into a single simultaneous operating point.

Match the scope of the replacement

For a piston-related order, provide the installed liner diameter, piston construction, rod interface and flushing arrangement. For a hydraulic-block enquiry, include valve-cover, manifold and mounting photographs. Shaft replacements require mating dimensions, key details and the exact suffix on the drawing.

Information to include in a parts enquiry

  • Complete pump designation, manufacturer shown on the nameplate, serial number and year.
  • Original drawing or catalogue reference, including every suffix and the figure / position number.
  • Part photographs, key mating dimensions and the assembly in which the part is installed.
  • For fluid-end consumables: liner bore, piston construction, seal arrangement and required quantity.
  • Delivery destination and the list of parts required for the same maintenance job.
