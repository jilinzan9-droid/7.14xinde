ABRAJ Reports PDO Multi-Stage Fracturing Milestone at Oman's Nimr Field

Published by Pratt Oil · 15 October 2026
Original announcement: 2026-02-03

ABRAJ reported PDO's first multi-stage fracturing operation on a horizontal oil well at Nimr. Review the news and implications for pump-parts planning.

Discuss a Parts RequirementView Industry News

ABRAJ Energy Services announced on 3 February 2026 that it had completed PDO's first multi-stage hydraulic fracturing operation on a horizontal oil well at the Nimr field in Oman. The company also referred to the earlier deployment of its second fracturing fleet for PDO at Sayh An-Nihayda. These details appear in ABRAJ's original announcement.

Buyer analysis: look beyond the headline to equipment information

For buyers following this development, the useful distinction is between the scope of the stimulation service and the specification of the equipment performing it. A multi-stage operation describes how the well is treated; it does not identify the pump assembly or its wear parts. Any associated maintenance inquiry therefore needs a separate, equipment-specific scope.

Frac pump maintenance planning starts with the actual pump and the work identified by the maintenance team. A fleet-level description cannot confirm the fluid-end design, plunger size, packing arrangement or valve and seat reference fitted to an individual unit. Those details should come from equipment records and the relevant drawings, rather than assumptions based on a public project announcement.

Connect maintenance demand to a traceable parts list

Our editorial recommendation is to maintain a unit-specific list that distinguishes confirmed replacement requirements from items still awaiting inspection. Keep the pump nameplate, component part numbers and drawing revisions with the request. Record whether the enquiry concerns a complete assembly or individual wear parts, and make missing information visible before comparing offers.

Use product references without assuming project compatibility

Pratt Oil's fracturing pump parts overview can help buyers prepare a model-specific enquiry. Include the pump identification and requested components so the proposed scope can be reviewed. For a catalogue inquiry, keep the requested supply scope and the equipment records together.
