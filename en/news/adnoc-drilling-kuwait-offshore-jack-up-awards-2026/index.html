ADNOC Drilling Receives Kuwait Offshore Rig Awards

Published by Pratt Oil · 16 November 2026

ADNOC Drilling has announced approval of awards for two offshore jack-up rigs for Kuwait Oil Company (KOC). Its release, datelined 9 November 2026 and posted on its website on 10 November, puts the combined maximum contract value at approximately US$313.6 million. Operations are expected to begin in the fourth quarter of 2027.

What the announcement covers

The awards concern the provision and operation of two high-specification jack-up rigs. The stated firm term is four years, including mobilization, with a further one-year option. The maximum value excludes that optional year. ADNOC Drilling expects to use one rig it owns and bareboat a second.

The company describes the awards as its entry into offshore Kuwait and its first offshore rig operations outside the UAE. The awards remain subject to applicable conditions and contractual formalities. The expected start date is a forward-looking schedule, rather than confirmation that operations have already begun.

Source: ADNOC Drilling's official KOC jack-up rig announcement.

Procurement perspective: identify the installed equipment

The award announcement does not provide a mud pump, drawworks or brake bill of materials. Buyers preparing maintenance packages should obtain the actual equipment register and assembly drawings before selecting replacement parts. A rig description is useful project context, but it does not identify an individual component.

For a mud pump spare parts request, keep the manufacturer, full pump model, serial reference and assembly position together. For drawworks brakes and clutches, record the installed assembly model and the separate wear-part references. These are purchasing checks for a buyer's own equipment, not specifications published for the KOC rigs.

A practical request also separates commissioning requirements from later maintenance stock. List each part number, quantity, drawing revision and required delivery date on its own line. Ask the responsible equipment team to resolve unreadable markings or conflicting references before treating two entries as interchangeable.

The part and drawing-number reference can help locate existing catalogue entries. Any match still needs confirmation against the installed assembly and the requested scope.
