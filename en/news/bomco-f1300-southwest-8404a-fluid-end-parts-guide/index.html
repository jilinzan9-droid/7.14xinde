A BOMCO F-1300 fluid-end parts request should identify the installed configuration before using a catalog number. The supplied Southwest document covers its 8404A fluid end for the listed F-1300 and F-1600 applications. This guide focuses on replacement components for that configuration; it does not turn the source list into a universal bill of material for every F-1300 pump.

Define the module and retainer scope

In this reference, the suction module is 8404-2S and the discharge module is 8404-1D. The wearplate, module seal, liner-retainer flange and liner-retainer nut have separate numbers. The catalog also defines which item references are included in fluid-end assembly 8404A. A buyer should specify whether the request is for an individual module, a set of fitted components or the defined complete assembly.

The performance chart is equally specific. Its F-1300 row shows 5,000 psi with a 5-inch liner, 4,952 psi with a 5-1/4-inch liner and 2,789 psi with a 7-inch liner. The "5000 PSI" fluid-end heading therefore does not assign that pressure to every liner size. These are values from the supplied configuration chart, whose efficiency assumptions and equipment limits must remain part of any engineering review.

What to send with the parts enquiry

For an F-1300 parts quotation, provide the pump serial number, fluid-end identification, current drawing or catalog revision, part number, quantity and installed liner size. Photographs should show module markings and mating connections. List studs, nuts and seals separately when they are required, and state which items should be included in an assembly quote. Brand and source-part references help identify the requested component; they do not establish a manufacturing or authorization relationship with Pratt Oil.

For related purchasing information, see mud pump spare parts matching and liners, pistons, valves and seats. You can send your model and part references to Pratt Oil for component and document matching.
