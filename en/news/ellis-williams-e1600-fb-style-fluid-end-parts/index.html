Ellis Williams E-1600 FB-Style Fluid-End Parts Identification

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Identify Ellis Williams E-1600 FB-style fluid-end references from the supplied drawing without assuming EMSCO FB-1600 compatibility.

Find a part or drawing numberSearch all part references

Use the identity shown in the drawing title block

The supplied E1600 file is a single engineering drawing titled E-1600 FB STYLE FLUID END ASSEMBLY. Its title block identifies Ellis Williams Engineering. It is not an EMSCO F-1600 or FB-1600 parts list. The phrase FB style describes the source drawing and must not be turned into a claim that the illustrated parts fit an EMSCO FB-1600 pump. Keep the equipment identity, assembly title and installed markings together during a matching review.

The drawing covers a fluid end, not the complete pump

The source shows a sectional fluid-end illustration, an item list and separate size tables. It does not provide a complete pump performance sheet in this file. Horsepower, speed, stroke, delivery and operating-pressure claims therefore cannot be derived from the E-1600 name or the drawing alone. Use the document to identify the illustrated fluid-end components, and request the equipment data and applicable assembly revision when assessing the actual installed configuration.

Preserve the size suffix for liners and pistons

The lower tables distinguish five cylinder/liner and piston sizes: 5, 5-1/2, 6, 6-1/2 and 7 inches. The cylinder/liner series uses 1320730 with size suffixes -01 through -05, while the piston series uses 1320736 with corresponding suffixes. Dropping the suffix removes the size distinction. The source item descriptions are in Russian; the English descriptions here are translated identification aids. The exact number and a checked drawing location should accompany any translated purchasing description.

Prepare a matching enquiry

Provide the complete equipment nameplate, serial number, fluid-end drawing and exact part number including the size suffix. Mark the requested component on the drawing and state its quantity. Include photographs of the removed part, mating interfaces and existing markings. For a liner or piston, confirm the installed bore. If an EMSCO FB-1600 number is proposed as an alternative, keep it separate until a documented compatibility review establishes its suitability.

For a specific requirement, use mud pump spare parts enquiries. Request confirmation of the exact reference and configuration before accepting a quotation. A catalogue entry does not establish stock, delivery time, manufacturer authorization or suitability for a particular pump.
