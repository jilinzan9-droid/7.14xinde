EMSCO F-1600 Spare Parts: Preserve the Full Serial and Drawing Context

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Identify EMSCO F-1600 fluid-end references and preserve the catalogue serial exceptions for power-end parts.

Keep the manufacturer and F-series version explicit

An F-1600 enquiry should include the manufacturer identity, not only the number 1600. The supplied EMSCO catalogue identifies an F-1300/F-1600 fluid end as 6316-0100-00, Revision P. Separate FA and FB sections appear elsewhere in the same book. This source does not establish compatibility with every pump sold under a similar F1600 name. Identify the actual equipment and installed assembly before selecting a replacement number from a general search result.

The crosshead index is not a simple serial cutoff

The F-1600 crosshead entries illustrate why serial details should be preserved exactly. The earlier index lists serial numbers 1 through 38 and 42. Its continuation lists 39, 41, 43, and 45 and up for another section. Do not rewrite these as one continuous early-versus-late range or guess how unlisted numbers should be treated. For an uncertain serial, review the equipment documentation and installed crosshead rather than assigning a section automatically.

Use the assembly drawing to identify the requested item

The fluid-end sheet separates the studded body, valve-pot cover, cylinder-head plug, cylinder head, thread ring and piston rod. Their numbers describe individual entries within the cited configuration. When the requirement is only a plug or retaining component, the complete fluid-end reference is useful context but is not the requested component number. For pistons, the drawing calls for a separate specification, so bore and existing part markings remain part of a meaningful enquiry.

Prepare a matching enquiry

Send the manufacturer name, complete F-1600 serial number, assembly number and exact part reference with quantity. Include the source drawing revision and photographs of the installed component. State whether the requirement concerns the fluid end or the power end. Keep any BOMCO, FA or FB alternative separate until its matching basis is confirmed. A clear serial photograph is preferable to an abbreviated range copied from a previous purchasing list.

For a specific requirement, use mud pump spare parts enquiries. Request confirmation of the exact reference and configuration before accepting a quotation. A catalogue entry does not establish stock, delivery time, manufacturer authorization or suitability for a particular pump.
