EMSCO F-500 Spare Parts: Match the Revision-L Fluid-End Column

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Use the EMSCO F-500 fluid-end column to distinguish assembly, piston-rod and cylinder-head references.

A shared catalogue page is not one universal assembly

The F-series parts book places the EMSCO F-500 fluid end beside F-350 and F-650 references. That layout helps compare component descriptions, but it does not make the three complete assemblies interchangeable. PDF page 12 has separate model columns and revision labels. For an F-500 requirement, read the F-500 column first and carry its assembly reference into the enquiry. Checking the column avoids attaching a familiar description to a neighbouring model number.

Separate the assembly from the replacement item

The F-500 fluid-end assembly on this sheet is 6322-0034-00, Revision L. Its piston rod is 6322-0054-00. Several other entries on the same sheet share numbers across columns, including the studded fluid end and cylinder-head components. Shared entries are evidence about those particular catalogue rows only. They do not establish that every F-350, F-500 or F-650 wear part, liner bore or later drawing revision is a suitable alternative.

Make the requested scope clear

A request for a fluid end may mean a complete assembly, a studded body, or a small group of service components. These are different purchasing scopes. Identify whether the removed part is the cylinder head, cylinder-head plug, thread ring or piston rod, and retain its number exactly as printed. Photographs of threads, seating faces and connection details support matching when a marking is damaged. Dimensions supplement the original drawing; they should not silently replace a clear manufacturer reference.

Prepare a matching enquiry

Prepare one enquiry line for each F-500 item, showing the exact number, quantity and assembly reference. Add the serial number and drawing revision if available. For a complete fluid-end requirement, describe what must be included rather than assuming the assembly name defines the supply scope. For a liner or piston requirement, provide the installed bore, related part markings and photographs. Ask for written confirmation of the matched configuration before the order is agreed.

For a specific requirement, use mud pump spare parts enquiries. Request confirmation of the exact reference and configuration before accepting a quotation. A catalogue entry does not establish stock, delivery time, manufacturer authorization or suitability for a particular pump.
