EMSCO FB-1600 Part Numbers: Match the General Assembly and Power End

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Trace EMSCO FB-1600 general-assembly, crankshaft, pinion and crosshead references before requesting replacement parts.

Use the FB-1600 master drawing to locate the assembly

The supplied FB-1600 parts list includes a general-assembly drawing identified as PL 6316-0374-00, Revision F. Its bill of materials separates the frame, crankshaft, pinion shaft, crosshead, fluid end and lubrication assembly. This gives a useful route from a pump model to the correct part-list section. It does not mean that the general-assembly number identifies every loose component or that every illustrated item is included in a particular quotation.

Keep power-end numbers attached to their descriptions

On PDF page 3, 6316-0099-00 identifies the crankshaft assembly and 6316-0098-00 the pinion-shaft assembly. The crosshead assembly is 6316-0183-00. These references should not be shortened to a similar string of digits without a description. Preserve the full number, including its final suffix, and distinguish a complete assembly from a bearing, retaining item or other component within it. A photograph and marked drawing help clarify an enquiry when an existing marking is unclear.

A shared fluid-end reference has a limited meaning

The FB-1600 source lists fluid-end assembly 6316-0375-00, which also appears in the supplied FB-1300 documentation. That is evidence about the cited assembly reference, not proof that every part of the two pump models is interchangeable. The FB-1600 general assembly and power-end requirements still need their own matching context. If an enquiry combines both models, keep the equipment identity and requested quantity for each item clearly separated.

Prepare a matching enquiry

Send the FB-1600 nameplate and serial number, exact part number, required quantity and applicable drawing revision. Identify whether the request is for a complete assembly or an individual component inside it. Include photographs of the installed part and its markings, and flag any previous rebuild or configuration change. If a number has been proposed from an FB-1300 list, ask for the specific matching basis rather than assuming model-wide interchangeability.

For a specific requirement, use FB-1600 parts reference page, mud pump spare parts enquiries. Request confirmation of the exact reference and configuration before accepting a quotation. A catalogue entry does not establish stock, delivery time, manufacturer authorization or suitability for a particular pump.

Document basis

This technical purchasing guide is based on EMSCO FB1600 PARTS LIST.pdf (PDF pages 1, 3, 4). Source page numbers follow the referenced document’s PDF page order. The table is a selected, checked reference rather than a complete parts list or an operating instruction.

Mud King catalogue references for this model

Keep those prefixes and the stated size, valve series, liner outside diameter and source table together. They are not presented as original pump-manufacturer numbers or as a confirmation of fit.
