EMSCO FC-1600 Parts: Check the Low-Temperature 5000 PSI Drawing Version

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Use the documented EMSCO FC-1600 low-temperature fluid-end reference and confirm drawing suffixes before ordering.

Find a part or drawing numberSearch all part references

Match the documented FC-1600 configuration

The supplied twenty-page FC-1600 parts list contains a fluid-end drawing titled for an FC1600 low-temperature, 5000 PSI configuration. Its reference is 6316-0617-00. This identifies the configuration shown in that document; it is not a universal pressure or temperature statement for every FC-1600 pump or replacement assembly. A purchasing enquiry should retain the model, drawing number and relevant configuration notes so that the installed equipment can be checked against the source.

Distinguish the general assembly from the fluid-end modules

The general-assembly bill identifies reference 6316-0618-00 and directs the fluid-end requirement to 6316-0617-00. The fluid-end sheet then separates discharge and suction studded modules, a threaded retainer, thread ring, wear plate and other components. These are different purchasing scopes. A reference to the complete pump or fluid end can provide context, but the requested loose component still needs its own exact number and quantity.

Do not merge conflicting drawing endings

The supplied source shows 6316-0610-00 for the discharge studded fluid end. A number ending -01 in another list should be treated as a separate reference until the drawing revision and configuration are compared. Removing punctuation for searching can help locate candidates, but it must not change the number quoted or ordered. Selected references here are not a complete bill of materials. Review the applicable assembly drawing before selecting additional components.

Prepare a matching enquiry

Provide the FC-1600 serial number, installed assembly reference, source drawing revision and exact component number. Include the operating configuration for review rather than selecting a pressure label from a search result. Attach photographs of the module markings and mating interfaces, and state whether the requirement is one module, a complete fluid end or associated parts. Keep -00 and -01 variants separate until their suitability has been explicitly confirmed.

For a specific requirement, use FC-1600 parts reference page; compare its listed edition, mud pump spare parts enquiries. Request confirmation of the exact reference and configuration before accepting a quotation. A catalogue entry does not establish stock, delivery time, manufacturer authorization or suitability for a particular pump.
