EMSCO FC-2200 Parts: Trace the 7500 PSI General-Assembly Drawing

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Trace the EMSCO FC-2200 general-assembly drawing to its frame, crankshaft, pinion, crosshead and fluid-end references.

Read the configuration note with the drawing number

The supplied FC-2200 document is a National-Oilwell parts list identified as EPL 1345, dated December 2000. Its general-assembly drawing is labelled 7500 P.S.I. and carries reference PL 6323-0110-10. That pressure description belongs to the illustrated configuration. It should not be presented as a guarantee for every FC-2200 pump, every installed manifold or any proposed replacement part. The drawing is an identification source, not an instruction to operate equipment at a selected pressure.

Follow the bill of materials to the required assembly

The general-assembly bill on PDF page 3 lists separate references for the frame, crankshaft, pinion shaft, crosshead and fluid end. For example, 6323-0080-00 identifies the crankshaft assembly and 6323-0022-00 the pinion-shaft assembly. Keep the description with the number to prevent a request for an assembly being mistaken for one component within it. A marked drawing is particularly useful when a maintenance request refers only to a position or photograph.

Retain the FC prefix and exact suffixes

FC-2200 should remain a complete model identity in a parts enquiry. The source does not establish that a similarly named F, FA, FB or FC-1600 item is an interchangeable replacement. Individual catalogue references and final suffixes need to be compared in their assembly context. Do not infer horsepower, stroke or other operating ratings from the model digits when the cited drawing does not supply that specification. Request the applicable equipment data and installed component details instead.

Prepare a matching enquiry

Send the full FC-2200 model and serial number, exact reference and required quantity. Attach the relevant drawing revision and photographs of the installed assembly or component. State the actual requirement as an individual item or a defined assembly scope, and include the equipment configuration for review. Where a proposed alternative comes from a different pump family, ask for its matching basis explicitly instead of accepting a similar-looking description.

For a specific requirement, use mud pump spare parts enquiries. Request confirmation of the exact reference and configuration before accepting a quotation. A catalogue entry does not establish stock, delivery time, manufacturer authorization or suitability for a particular pump.
