F1600 Mud Pump Spare Parts

Published by Pratt Oil · Updated 14 October 2026

Replacement parts supply and matching for F1600 mud pump maintenance. Send your nameplate, part number or drawing for quotation support.

WhatsApp inquiryCompany News

Pratt Oil supplies replacement spare parts for F1600 mud pumps. Use this guide to identify the part family and prepare the details needed for parts matching and a quotation.

Parts for F1600 mud pump maintenance

Start with the component you need to replace. The linked catalog pages show general part references; the correct item for your pump must be confirmed from its nameplate and parts information.

  • Cylinder liners: provide the liner bore, part number and drawing or dimensions of the existing liner.
  • Mud pump pistons: include the piston assembly reference and the liner bore it works with.
  • Valves and valve components: identify the required valve and seat references, with clear photos if the part number is unavailable.
  • Piston rod extensions: send the drawing, connection details and component part number.
  • Hydraulic cylinder assemblies: confirm the assembly reference and mark the required item on the parts drawing.
  • Seals, gaskets, springs and covers: list each requested item separately with its drawing position, part number and quantity.

Confirm the pump model before ordering

Send a clear nameplate photo showing the manufacturer and full model marking, including F-1600 if that is how it is written on your equipment. Keep any suffixes in the inquiry. If the nameplate specifies F1600HL or FB1600, include that exact reference when requesting parts.

The model name alone does not establish that a particular liner, valve, rod or assembly will fit. Use the manufacturer's parts list, component number, drawing and dimensions to confirm the requested replacement. For an FB1600 nameplate, refer to our FB1600 spare parts information.

Information for an F1600 spare parts quotation

  1. Pump identification: manufacturer, full model and nameplate photo.
  2. Part identification: OEM or reference number, drawing position and clear component photos.
  3. Matching details: dimensions, liner bore and any material or specification requirement stated in your parts documentation.
  4. Order details: quantity for each item, delivery destination and required delivery date.

If you do not have a part number, send the available photos, drawing or dimensions and identify the component you need. Matching should be reviewed before an order is confirmed.
