Separate the complete rod assembly from its components

The ordering page lists two-piece studded piston rod assembly 6284-25A separately from piston rod 6278-25, connecting stud 1290-2 and sub rod 6284-1. A request for a "piston rod assembly" should state whether all of those components are required. Photographs and the existing rod markings help distinguish an individual replacement rod from a complete assembly or an alternative fitted arrangement.

Liner references are also tied to both bore and material. The ordering chart lists 6390G1 and 6390ZIR1 for 5-inch GRAYLOY and zirconia liners, and different references for 6-inch and 7-inch entries. Keep the size and material alongside the number. In the source F-1600 pressure chart, 6-inch and 7-inch liners are shown at 4,665 and 3,423 psi respectively; the 5,000-psi heading cannot be applied to every bore.

What to send with the parts enquiry

To prepare an F1600 parts request, send the nameplate, serial number, installed fluid-end identification, source drawing and original part number with quantity. Add liner bore, material, rod configuration and clear photographs of markings and connections. Keep F1600 distinct from FB1600 and F1600HL when describing the equipment. The F1600 spare parts page provides the model-specific enquiry route; any alternative reference still needs confirmation against the installed assembly.

For related purchasing information, see mud pump spare parts matching and liners, pistons, valves and seats. You can send your model and part references to Pratt Oil for component and document matching.
