Honghua 3NB-1000F Mud Pump Parts and Drawing References

Published by Pratt Oil · 21 October 2026

Identify Honghua 3NB-1000F material codes and component scope before requesting fluid-end, piston, liner or power-end replacement parts.

Honghua 3NB-1000F replacement-part identification requires the source catalogue as well as the model. The bilingual material-code book uses ten-digit identifiers for assemblies, wear parts and bought-in components. Preserve those codes as printed; a similarly named component in another 1000F parts book may use a different drawing-number family.

Read the assembly and material-code scope together

The 1000F fluid-end assembly table lists 1200008649 for the discharge manifold. Its cylinder-detail table separately identifies 1200008655 for the clamp assembly and 1200008651 for the piston rod. This is a useful example of why a complete assembly line and an individual component line should remain separate in a request for quotation.

Configuration details to confirm

The piston charts include some 800F/1000F application groups alongside other model groups. Retain the exact component type, printed size and application cell when selecting a reference. A code listed for a piston hub is not a substitute for the rubber, plate or snap-ring code, even where a diameter is repeated.

Information for a replacement-parts enquiry

Provide a readable nameplate photograph, pump serial number, the catalogue cover or revision, the material code, source page, item number and required quantity. Add photographs of the removed component and its markings. For threaded parts, include the thread form and measured dimensions; for seals, include the actual sealing profile as well as the nominal size.

The reference list distinguishes a complete assembly from its individual components. State whether the request concerns one component, a matched set or a complete assembly, and identify any parts that must remain paired. Codes printed for standards, equipment models or consumable grades have a different role from a custom part identifier.

For related selection information, see our mud pump spare-parts supply guide. Send the model and component details through the Pratt Oil contact page for a quotation review. Supply and suitability require confirmation against the submitted details.

Identify the 2011 NB1000G-TC catalogue revision

The Honghua parts list marked 3NB-1000F, document NB1000G-TC, is dated November 2011. Its drawing-reference system includes NB1000G assemblies and NB800G components. A component carrying an NB800G prefix is therefore not automatically the wrong item for this manual: several such references are expressly listed in its 3NB-1000F fluid-end and general-assembly tables. Match the complete reference, component description and assembly position before ordering.

Power-end references that need their full suffix

The crankshaft table identifies NB1000G.02.10B as the widened bull gear. The pinion-shaft table likewise identifies NB1000G.03.02B as the widened pinion shaft. Retain the B suffix in an enquiry and provide the fitted gear dimensions or drawing. These entries do not establish that a different suffix or an unsuffixed gear is interchangeable. The same section distinguishes right and left main-bearing sleeves, bearing end covers and positioning rings.

Fluid-end and valve matching

The source lists NB800G.05.01.00 for the fluid-cylinder assembly and NB800G.05.17.00 for the valve assembly. Its API 6# notation describes that valve entry; it is not evidence of a supplier certification. The piston NB1000G.05.02.00, piston rod NB1000G.05.03, bimetal liner NB800G.05.22 and standard liner NB800G.05.22A remain separate references. Confirm liner bore, piston arrangement, seal profile and the removed component rather than selecting from the pump name alone.

The general table also distinguishes discharge-elbow assembly NB1000G.15.00 from discharge five-way-cross assembly NB1000G.15.00A. State which arrangement is fitted. Catalogue quantities describe the illustrated assembly, not stock availability or a recommended spare quantity.

Prepare a matching enquiry

Send a nameplate photograph, this catalogue revision, the full drawing reference, quantity required, clear photographs and measured mating dimensions. Keep the 2011 drawing references separate from ten-digit material codes in later catalogues until an explicit cross-reference is confirmed. Use our mud pump spare parts enquiry page to request a review of the supplied identification details.
