Honghua 3NB-1300F Mud Pump Parts and Drawing References

Published by Pratt Oil · 21 October 2026

Identify Honghua 3NB-1300F material codes and component scope before requesting fluid-end, piston, liner or power-end replacement parts.

A Honghua 3NB-1300F parts list contains both shared 1300F/1600F fluid-end items and model-specific power-end references. Keep these two scopes separate when preparing an enquiry. The material code, assembly location and pump identification are all needed to review whether a proposed replacement matches the installed configuration.

Read the assembly and material-code scope together

The shared fluid-cylinder detail identifies 1200004213 for the clamp assembly,1200004187 for the piston rod and 1200004179 for the liner seal ring. The crankshaft section then gives a dedicated 1300F table, including 1200003790 for its bull gear. A shared cylinder illustration therefore does not establish that the rotating components are shared too.

Configuration details to confirm

For gears, connecting rods and bearings, send the assembly drawing, markings and the mating-part details. For fluid-end wear parts, add the installed liner or piston size and sealing arrangement. Keep the 1300F designation in the enquiry rather than shortening it to 1300 or using an HHF model label from another catalogue.

Information for a replacement-parts enquiry

Provide a readable nameplate photograph, pump serial number, the catalogue cover or revision, the material code, source page, item number and required quantity. Add photographs of the removed component and its markings. For threaded parts, include the thread form and measured dimensions; for seals, include the actual sealing profile as well as the nominal size.

The reference list distinguishes a complete assembly from its individual components. State whether the request concerns one component, a matched set or a complete assembly, and identify any parts that must remain paired. Codes printed for standards, equipment models or consumable grades have a different role from a custom part identifier.

For related selection information, see our mud pump spare-parts supply guide. Send the model and component details through the Pratt Oil contact page for a quotation review. Supply and suitability require confirmation against the submitted details.
