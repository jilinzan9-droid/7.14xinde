Keep valve-reference suffixes intact

The fluid-end continuation includes valve assembly NB2200G.05.31.00 and a separately printed NB2200G.05.31(A).00. Preserve the (A) notation when it appears on the equipment documentation. Do not remove it or assume that the two assemblies can be substituted.
