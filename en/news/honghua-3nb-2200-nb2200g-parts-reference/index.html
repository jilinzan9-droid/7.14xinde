Honghua 3NB-2200 Parts: NB2200G Drawing-Number Guide

Published by Pratt Oil · 21 October 2026

Identify Honghua 3NB-2200 spare parts using NB2200G drawing numbers, cylinder and valve references, and the exact parts-book configuration.

The supplied Honghua 3NB-2200 parts book identifies the pump as 3NB-2200 and the catalogue as NB2200G-TC, dated November 2012. Its general assembly is NB2200G.00. Use those printed identifiers to establish the reference configuration before ordering parts.

The cover does not identify an HL variant. Confirm any such configuration using the corresponding nameplate and parts catalogue. Confirm the pump nameplate and applicable parts book before treating the references in this guide as candidates for your equipment.

NB800G and GH3161 identifiers also appear in individual rows of this NB2200G catalogue. Preserve the exact printed prefix; do not change every reference to NB2200G to make the list look uniform.

Discharge and suction cylinder assemblies are separate items

The fluid-end list names discharge-cylinder assembly NB2200G.05.02.00 and suction-cylinder assembly NB2200G.05.38.00. Their detailed parts tables begin with discharge cylinder NB2200G.05.02.01 and suction cylinder NB2200G.05.38.01.

The same source lists piston NB2200G.05.09.00, standard liner NB2200G.05.11, bimetallic liner NB2200G.05.12, and liner retaining gland NB2200G.05.21.00. These references identify catalogue entries; they do not replace confirmation of the required bore, dimensions, material and seal arrangement.

Keep valve-reference suffixes intact

The fluid-end continuation includes valve assembly NB2200G.05.31.00 and a separately printed NB2200G.05.31(A).00. Preserve the (A) notation when it appears on the equipment documentation. Do not remove it or assume that the two assemblies can be substituted.

Related entries include valve spring NB2200G.05.33, valve-stem guide NB2200G.05.34.00 and valve-cover seal NB2200G.05.37. Provide the relevant exploded-view item number to distinguish a complete valve assembly from an individual wear part.

Check the scope of ancillary equipment

The book also shows a KB-45H pulsation-dampener assembly under NB2200G.24.00 and a JA-3H shear-pin safety valve under NB2200G.27.00. Those are source-specific equipment descriptions. They are not evidence that another pump has the same dampener, connection or safety-valve installation.

Information needed for a precise parts request

  • Pump model, serial number and nameplate photograph.
  • NB2200G-TC cover or another applicable catalogue revision.
  • The complete drawing number, including suffixes and punctuation.
  • Assembly name, exploded-view item number and quantity.
  • For wear parts: required size, actual-part photographs and measured dimensions.
  • For connection or safety-system parts: connection details and the applicable equipment documentation.

Our mud pump spare-parts supply information explains the details to include in a quotation request. Final selection should follow the installed configuration, rather than a similar model name or an isolated part photograph.
