Honghua 3NB-500F Parts: Liners, Valves and Material Codes

Published by Pratt Oil · 21 October 2026

Identify Honghua 3NB-500F material codes and component scope before requesting fluid-end, piston, liner or power-end replacement parts.

The Honghua 3NB-500F material-code catalogue separates the complete fluid cylinder, the valve components and the size-dependent liner and piston entries. A description such as 500F repair kit leaves several of these choices unresolved. Use the actual component code and the relevant table item when preparing a maintenance purchase list.

Read the assembly and material-code scope together

The fluid-end assembly list identifies 1200009392 as a fluid-cylinder assembly, while the cylinder detail lists 1200009460 for the clamp assembly and 1200009445 for the piston rod. The liner row on the detail page has no code of its own; the following size chart supplies separate bi-metal-liner codes. This distinction matters when the requirement is a sized wear part rather than the complete cylinder assembly.

Configuration details to confirm

The piston-component charts also distinguish the piston hub, rubber, retainer plate and snap ring. A shared diameter does not make these four components the same purchasing item. Send the installed piston construction and photographs of the removed parts with any request for a replacement set.

Information for a replacement-parts enquiry

Provide a readable nameplate photograph, pump serial number, the catalogue cover or revision, the material code, source page, item number and required quantity. Add photographs of the removed component and its markings. For threaded parts, include the thread form and measured dimensions; for seals, include the actual sealing profile as well as the nominal size.

The reference list distinguishes a complete assembly from its individual components. State whether the request concerns one component, a matched set or a complete assembly, and identify any parts that must remain paired. Codes printed for standards, equipment models or consumable grades have a different role from a custom part identifier.

For related selection information, see our mud pump spare-parts supply guide. Send the model and component details through the Pratt Oil contact page for a quotation review. Supply and suitability require confirmation against the submitted details.
