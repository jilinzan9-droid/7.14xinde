Honghua 3NB-800F Mud Pump Parts and Drawing References

Published by Pratt Oil · 21 October 2026

Identify Honghua 3NB-800F material codes and component scope before requesting fluid-end, piston, liner or power-end replacement parts.

For a Honghua 3NB-800F parts request, keep the model, material code and assembly description together. The bilingual catalogue provides a dedicated 800F fluid-end list and separate pages for piston sizes, crankshaft components, the crosshead, pinion shaft and auxiliary systems. These sections support a more precise enquiry than a model name followed by a general request for spare parts.

Read the assembly and material-code scope together

The 800F fluid-end list shows 1200010143 for the fluid-cylinder assembly and 1200010241 for the liner end cover. The cylinder detail separates 1200010239 for the clamp assembly from 1200010226 for the piston rod. Related liner and piston charts use their own size rows, so the complete cylinder reference should not replace the required wear-part reference.

Configuration details to confirm

Several application-chart cells cover both 800F and 1000F, but other rows cover 800F alone. The merged application cell is part of the identification evidence. Keep its size range and component family with the code instead of assuming that every 800F item is shared with 1000F.

Information for a replacement-parts enquiry

Provide a readable nameplate photograph, pump serial number, the catalogue cover or revision, the material code, source page, item number and required quantity. Add photographs of the removed component and its markings. For threaded parts, include the thread form and measured dimensions; for seals, include the actual sealing profile as well as the nominal size.

The reference list distinguishes a complete assembly from its individual components. State whether the request concerns one component, a matched set or a complete assembly, and identify any parts that must remain paired. Codes printed for standards, equipment models or consumable grades have a different role from a custom part identifier.

For related selection information, see our mud pump spare-parts supply guide. Send the model and component details through the Pratt Oil contact page for a quotation review. Supply and suitability require confirmation against the submitted details.

Honghua 3NB-800F mud pump parts should be matched to the installed pump and its catalogue revision. The supplied Russian-language catalogue identifies the model as 3NB-800F and the book as NB800G-TC, November 2012. Its individual references use the NB800G drawing family. Keep that source identity with a parts enquiry rather than treating all 800-class mud pumps as one interchangeable specification.

Start with the relevant assembly

The catalogue separates the frame, crankshaft, pinion shaft, crosshead, fluid end, power-end lubrication and liner-wash systems. It also has dedicated discharge-strainer, discharge-connection and KB-45 dampener sections. Match the assembly drawing and its numbered item before choosing an individual component. A number found in a different pump family is not a substitute for the number printed on the installed pump's drawing.

Keep the A and B drawing suffixes

The pinion-shaft table separately lists NB800G.03.02, NB800G.03.02A and NB800G.03.02B. The crankshaft section likewise distinguishes NB800G.02.10.00, NB800G.02.10A and NB800G.02.10B gear references. The source describes different tooth-processing variants. These are separate catalogue entries, so deleting the final letter can remove a useful selection distinction. Confirm the installed gear and pinion combination from the relevant component drawings; this reference list does not establish that the variants are interchangeable.

Liners and sealing parts need more than a family number

The fluid-end table lists bimetal liner family NB800G.05.22 separately from standard liner family NB800G.05.22A. It also gives distinct references for the piston, piston rod, seals and valve assembly. Include the actual liner bore, piston construction, seal arrangement and photographs with the drawing number. Do not extend a source-listed reference to an unlisted size.

Standard fasteners, tubing and O-rings appear alongside custom drawing numbers. A GB/T designation identifies a standard family; the source dimension or thread information and the assembly item identify the particular requirement. Preserve both thread ends and overall length when requesting studs, and the complete source size for sealing rings.

Prepare a clear 3NB-800F parts request

Provide the nameplate, serial number, catalogue cover, assembly page, full drawing number, item number, quantity and installed-part photographs. Add measurements and material or seal requirements where applicable. Some descriptions in the supplied translated catalogue are incomplete; those entries require clarification before a component identity can be confirmed. Source quantities describe the historical assembly and do not indicate available stock.

See our mud pump spare-parts supply information and include the NB800G source revision, destination and required documentation with your enquiry. Suitability and availability must be confirmed against the submitted information.
