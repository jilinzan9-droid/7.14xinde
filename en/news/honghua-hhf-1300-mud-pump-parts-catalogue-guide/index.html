Identify the HHF-1300 connecting rod

The English comparison table on PDF page 40 lists connecting rod 11-3131-0206 with drawing reference GH3131-02.06. It places 6317-0026-00 in the FB counterpart column. These are three fields in a specific historical comparison, rather than three universally interchangeable order numbers.

Include a photograph of the installed rod and the relevant assembly drawing. State whether the requirement is for the rod, a bearing, associated fasteners or an assembly. Describe any modifications visible on the removed component. If an enquiry begins with a counterpart number, send the full comparison row instead of dropping the HHF and drawing identifiers.

Keep the bearing suffixes and specifications

The same HHF-1300 page gives 11-3131-0221-1 for the eccentric-strap bearing, with designation 928/660.4QU, and 11-3131-0222-1 for the main bearing, with designation 3G4053160H. The terminal -1 is part of each catalogue reference. The HHF-1600 comparison uses different entries, so do not remove the suffix when copying a parts list.

For a bearing enquiry, provide the markings on the actual bearing, its position in the assembly and the required quantity. Where available, attach the dimensional drawing and the existing bearing identification. The source specification helps identify the request; it does not confirm the suitability of an unreviewed replacement.

A different drawing prefix can still occur in this source

The HHF-1300 comparison also lists hollow crankshaft 11-3161-0208-00, drawing GH3161-02.08.00. Preserve that printed prefix. Do not automatically change every 3161 or GH3161 reference into 3131 simply because the pump model is HHF-1300. The source page and component description determine the documented association.

The English catalogue notes that its compared gear components are used as a matched pair. It also identifies fluid-end screw-hole and piston-rod-clamp differences. Those qualifications belong to that catalogue configuration. They do not establish compatibility for all production revisions, and they are not a statement of original-manufacturer authorisation.
