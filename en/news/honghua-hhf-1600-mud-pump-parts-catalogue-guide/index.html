For Honghua HHF-1600 spare parts, distinguish a complete cylinder assembly from the individual fluid-end block, liner-retaining component, piston rod or seal. The legacy parts books use related drawing families for several assemblies, but that does not make their component numbers equivalent. This guide keeps the original references and comparison notes available for a component-specific enquiry.

Cylinder assembly and fluid-end block are separate entries

PDF page 43 of the English comparison catalogue lists cylinder-block assembly 11-3161-0501-00, drawing GH3161-05.01.00, separately from the fluid-end component 11-3161-0501-01, drawing GH3161-05.01.01. The ending -00 or -01 therefore matters when describing the requested item. Include the item callout and an assembly photograph to distinguish the assembly from its individual block.

That page also identifies cylinder cover 11-3161-0502 and its seal ring 11-3161-0508. A request for a cover should specify whether the associated retaining and sealing parts are needed. For any pressure-containing component, send the applicable equipment documentation and component drawing so that the actual arrangement can be reviewed.

Keep liner-retaining parts and piston-rod clamps distinct

The liner-retainer ring is listed as 11-3161-0516, while the liner-retainer screw is 11-3161-0517-00. Piston rod 11-3161-0518 and clamp assembly 11-3161-0522-00 are separate references. Retain the liner size, piston construction and connection details with the relevant drawing number; a model name does not establish every wear-part size.

The catalogue's FB comparison includes a directional qualification for the piston-rod clamp: its note describes an FB clamp application on HHF, while the reverse arrangement lacks the spray-line connection thread. This is a historical source statement for that comparison. It is not a blanket fit claim for every HHF-1600 or FB-1600 installation. Supply photographs of the actual clamp and wash-line connection before selecting a counterpart.

HHF-1600 bearing references have their own suffixes

The HHF-1600 comparison on PDF page 41 lists connecting rod 11-3161-0206, eccentric-strap bearing 11-3161-0221-2 with designation 929/660.4QU, and main bearing 11-3161-0222-2 with designation 3G3003760HY. Preserve the complete -2 ending and bearing designation. The separate HHF-1300 comparison has different bearing entries.

Where two references appear for an item in the shared main catalogue, use the model-specific comparison page and installed component markings to identify the documented alternative. Do not interpret a neighbouring number or a similar prefix as an automatic replacement or supersession.

Prepare an HHF-1600 component list for review

Send the nameplate, serial number, catalogue cover or revision, assembly page and exact component reference. Add the quantity, delivery destination and photographs of the removed part and its mating connections. For liners and seals, include the size and sealing profile; for studs and nuts, include thread forms and length.
