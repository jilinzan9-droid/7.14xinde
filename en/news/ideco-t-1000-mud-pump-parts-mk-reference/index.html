IDECO T-1000 Mud Pump Parts: Rod, Liner and Module-Hardware Identification

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Identify IDECO T-1000 liners, valve-service components and supplier references with source-page context before preparing a parts enquiry.

Selected drawing referencesPart Number ReferenceMud pump spare parts

For a IDECO T-1000 mud pump parts enquiry, the complete component reference is more useful than the pump model alone. The Mud King Products catalogue separates expendable parts by nominal size, component family and installed arrangement. This guide transcribes the identifiable supplier-number entries associated with IDECO T-1000, with their original page and table context, so a buyer can assemble a clearer maintenance RFQ.

Rod, Liner and Module-Hardware Identification

The T-1000 expendables table uses the MK-0230 liner family and MKG-0783 packing. In its rod table, however, the extension-rod reference is MK-16-0231, while the piston rod is MK-0230 and the clamp is MK-24-0230. Preserve that exact extension-rod entry rather than copying a T-800 list with a similar liner family.

A shared T-500/T-800/T-1000 module section identifies MK-551-458 and provides separate references for threaded rings, plugs, guides, bushings and manifold seals. These source items should remain distinguishable in an RFQ. A demand described only as a complete fluid-end kit needs clarification of which module, fittings and wear components the buyer expects to receive.

Read the catalogue reference correctly

The accompanying reference list covers the identifiable number-bearing cells in the relevant source tables, not a complete original-manufacturer bill of materials. MK and MKG identify supplier catalogue references; their prefixes, punctuation and combined suffixes are retained. A value marked N/A or left blank is not replaced by an invented number. A shared model heading records the catalogue scope and does not by itself confirm interchangeability on an individual pump.

Information to send for parts matching

Send the pump nameplate and serial number, full reference including its prefix, required quantity and a photograph of the installed or removed component. For liners and pistons, add the nominal size and sealing arrangement. For fluid-end hardware, identify the module configuration and whether an individual component or a studded assembly is required. Keep any original equipment drawing number as a separate field rather than stripping the supplier prefix to assume an OEM match.

Review mud pump spare parts matching, compare liners, pistons, valves and seats, or send Pratt Oil a parts list for review. Matching and availability require confirmation; these references do not establish authorisation, inventory or a delivery promise.
