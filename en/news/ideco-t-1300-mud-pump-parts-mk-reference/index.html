IDECO T-1300 Mud Pump Parts: Conventional and L-Shaped Fluid-End Parts

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Identify IDECO T-1300 liners, valve-service components and supplier references with source-page context before preparing a parts enquiry.

For a IDECO T-1300 mud pump parts enquiry, the complete component reference is more useful than the pump model alone. The Mud King Products catalogue separates expendable parts by nominal size, component family and installed arrangement. This guide transcribes the identifiable supplier-number entries associated with IDECO T-1300, with their original page and table context, so a buyer can assemble a clearer maintenance RFQ.

Conventional and L-Shaped Fluid-End Parts

The T-1300 sheet lists MK-0233-series liners, including MK-0233-70 for the 7-inch row, with MKG-0782 packing. The conventional module section is shared with T-1600 and identifies MK-551-122-01 for the fluid module. It lists threaded rings, valve guides, retainers and seals individually rather than treating every service item as part of one unnamed kit.

The L-shaped T-1300/T-1600 section is a separate arrangement. It lists MK-8407-7A for the complete assembly and distinguishes discharge and suction modules, studs, retainer parts and wear plates. A photograph or drawing of the installed fluid end is necessary to choose the correct section. The conventional and L-shaped references must not be treated as automatic substitutions.

Read the catalogue reference correctly

The accompanying reference list covers the identifiable number-bearing cells in the relevant source tables, not a complete original-manufacturer bill of materials. MK and MKG identify supplier catalogue references; their prefixes, punctuation and combined suffixes are retained. A value marked N/A or left blank is not replaced by an invented number. A shared model heading records the catalogue scope and does not by itself confirm interchangeability on an individual pump.

Information to send for parts matching

Send the pump nameplate and serial number, full reference including its prefix, required quantity and a photograph of the installed or removed component. For liners and pistons, add the nominal size and sealing arrangement. For fluid-end hardware, identify the module configuration and whether an individual component or a studded assembly is required. Keep any original equipment drawing number as a separate field rather than stripping the supplier prefix to assume an OEM match.

Review mud pump spare parts matching, compare liners, pistons, valves and seats, or send Pratt Oil a parts list for review. Matching and availability require confirmation; these references do not establish authorisation, inventory or a delivery promise.
