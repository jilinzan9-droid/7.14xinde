Mud Pump Bearing Numbers: What to Include in an Order

Published by Pratt Oil · 10 November 2026

A mud pump bearing order should contain two references when they are available: the pump drawing number and the complete bearing designation. Keep the bearing position, manufacturer marking and quantity beside them. This gives the supplier enough context to review the requested item without treating every bearing in the same pump as identical.

Keep the two numbering systems separate

The pump catalogue identifies where a bearing belongs in an assembly. The marking on the bearing or its packaging identifies the bearing itself. Copy both into your inquiry, with clear labels, even when one number seems more familiar.

For example, the Honghua 3NB-2200 NB2200G reference lists NB2200G.02.23 as a main bearing with designation 23172CA/C9W33. It separately lists NB2200G.03.12 as a cylindrical roller bearing, NU3152M/C4. These are different catalogue positions, not alternative numbers for the same component.

Retain every letter, slash and suffix

Write the complete designation exactly as it appears. Do not shorten NU3152M/C4 to NU3152 in a purchase line. If the catalogue and the installed bearing show different markings, send both rather than combining them into a new number.

NSK's bearing-designation guidance explains that supplementary codes describe features such as design, material or cage construction, and their meanings may depend on the manufacturer. It also notes that markings on a component can be shorter than the full designation on its packaging. A packaging label is therefore useful when available.

Identify the assembly position

State whether the request concerns a main bearing, eccentric bearing, pinion-shaft bearing or crosshead bearing. Mark the position on the relevant assembly drawing and include its title or revision. If you need a housing, sleeve, retaining ring or seal as well, list it separately with its own quantity.

The HHF-1300 drawing-number catalogue also contains several bearing positions. Use the catalogue for the installed pump model; the examples above apply to the stated NB2200G reference and do not establish compatibility with another pump.

Send a complete bearing inquiry

  • Pump manufacturer, full model and nameplate photo.
  • Assembly position and pump drawing number.
  • Complete bearing designation and visible manufacturer marking.
  • Photos of the markings and packaging label, plus any available drawing dimensions.
  • Quantity per reference and delivery destination.

Keep unclear characters marked as uncertain until a photograph or drawing confirms them. Pratt Oil can review this information with your parts request. Where a replacement is proposed, compare the complete stated designation and specifications before confirming the order.
