Mud Pump Crankshaft or Pinion: Assembly Order Scope

Published by Pratt Oil · 16 November 2026

A mud pump power-end quotation needs a clear supply scope: a complete crankshaft assembly, a gear-shaft assembly or individual components. The word “assembly” should be supported by a list of expected contents. Keep the exact pump model, catalogue column and drawing number beside each purchase line so the quotation can be checked against the installed equipment.

Keep the model column with the number

The F-series crankshaft assembly reference includes columns for F500, F800/F1000 and F1300/F1600 catalogue entries. It also preserves old and model-reference columns. A number copied without its column heading loses the context needed to review it.

For example, the published table includes AH36002-02.00 and AH1301020200 in its crank-assembly row. It lists gear-shaft assemblies on separate rows. Keep the original reference as printed and attach the relevant page; do not merge old and newer numbers into a new designation or infer an interchangeable replacement from their appearance.

List the expected assembly contents

For a complete assembly, specify whether the request includes the shaft, gear, bearings, sleeves, covers, seals and retaining components shown in your equipment drawing. Mark any parts that are expected to be reused rather than supplied. The supplier's quotation should make the included and separately quoted items clear.

For an individual replacement, use that component's own drawing number. Our mud pump pinion shaft reference includes shaft entries beside other power-end and fluid-end components. The presence of a component in a shared catalogue table does not make it part of every shaft order.

Retain suffixes and drawing revisions

Copy every letter, separator and suffix from the original drawing. Include the assembly revision and a nameplate photograph if the equipment documentation has more than one version. Photograph visible shaft, gear or bearing markings separately so a reviewer can distinguish the component reference from the pump's serial number.

If bearings are required, retain their full manufacturer designation as well as the pump drawing reference. The mud pump bearing-number guide explains why these two references serve different purposes in a purchase list.

Prepare a reviewable power-end RFQ

Send the manufacturer, complete pump model, serial number, catalogue title and revision, selected model column, exact drawing reference and quantity. Attach an assembly view with the requested components marked. Include available dimensional drawings and identify any uncertain readings without silently correcting them.

For an F1600 request, the F1600 spare parts page provides a model-specific starting point. Pratt Oil can review a clearly defined assembly or component list with your quotation request. Fit and final supply contents must be confirmed for the identified equipment before ordering.
