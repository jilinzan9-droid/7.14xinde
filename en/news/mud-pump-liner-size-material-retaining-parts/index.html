Mud Pump Liners: Bore Size, Material and Retaining Parts

Published by Pratt Oil · 10 November 2026

A mud pump liner inquiry should state the installed pump model, liner part number, bore size and required material. Also say whether the order covers the liner alone or includes seals and retaining parts. A pump model or nominal bore on its own leaves important details unresolved.

Copy the bore and its unit

Use the size shown in the applicable pump catalogue or liner drawing, and retain the unit. If your maintenance record uses inches and your purchase list uses millimetres, include the original size alongside any conversion. Do not replace a fractional inch size with an unlabelled rounded number.

In the Honghua HHF-1300/HHF-1600 catalogue reference, 11-3161-0521-22 is listed as a 5 1/2-inch bi-metal liner. The full number and size belong together in that catalogue entry. Confirm the model and configuration before using it in an order.

State the material requirement clearly

Our bimetal mud pump liner and ceramic mud pump liner pages represent separate product requests. Specify the material required by your equipment documentation or purchasing specification.

If you want quotations for both materials, ask for separate options with the same stated pump model, bore and quantity. Keep any proposed material change visible in the quotation and have the responsible equipment team review it. A shared bore size does not by itself establish that two liners have the same external geometry or mounting arrangement.

List retaining parts separately

The liner and surrounding hardware have different catalogue entries. For example, the HHF reference lists 11-3161-0516 as a liner flange, 11-3161-0517-00 as a liner retainer and 11-3161-0520 as a liner locking ring. Keep the relevant drawing page with these numbers, because catalogue descriptions can vary between versions.

State which of these items you need, with individual quantities. Do the same for seals or other parts shown in your assembly drawing. A request for “liner set” should include a list of its expected contents.

Send the details for matching

  • Pump manufacturer, complete model and readable nameplate photo.
  • Liner drawing number, bore size and unit.
  • Required material and any drawing revision.
  • Available overall length, external dimensions and flange or shoulder details from the drawing.
  • Quantity per liner size, additional parts and delivery destination.

Include the installed piston reference when it is available. Our piston and liner matching guide explains the related information to collect. Send separate order lines for different sizes or materials so the quotation can identify each requested option clearly.
