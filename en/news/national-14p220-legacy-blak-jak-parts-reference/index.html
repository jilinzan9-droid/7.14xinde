National 14-P-220 Parts: Checking Legacy and BLAK-JAK References

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Keep legacy EPL-955-1 and later 14-P-220 BLAK-JAK/HydraAlign part references separate. Compare valve-cover configurations before preparing an RFQ.

National 14-P-220 parts information in the reference catalogues covers more than one catalog generation. The legacy EPL-955-1 list and the later recommended-spares document identify different valve-cover and liner-retention arrangements. For procurement, the first task is to identify the equipment configuration and document revision; a newer-looking number is not automatically a replacement for an older part.

Separate catalog generations and assembly variants

The September 1995 EPL-955-1 catalog identifies studded and bare suction and discharge modules separately. The later 10640418-PAL Rev. 04 reference covers BLAK-JAK and HydraAlign-related components and includes a choice between non-tapered and tapered valve-cover arrangements. Buyers should therefore attach photographs of the installed cover, liner-retention equipment and rod arrangement when sending a 14-P-220 parts list.

A clear example is reference 1295174. The legacy page describes it as a seal retainer, while the later recommended-spares list calls it the non-tapered valve cover and lists its associated components. This difference needs the drawing context, not an automatic correction to either original document. The later tapered cover has its own reference, 80734011, with separately listed guide, insert and seal components.

The examples below are selected reference entries, not a complete bill of material or a confirmation of interchangeability.

Details to include with the parts request

A useful RFQ includes the model and serial number, source document and revision, assembly drawing, original number, description and quantity. Note whether the pump uses the legacy arrangement or the later retention and cover equipment. Keep numeric and alphanumeric references, suffixes and leading zeros unchanged. Use the 14-P-220 product reference for an initial enquiry, and provide the installed details before requesting a proposed alternative.

For related purchasing information, see mud pump spare parts matching and liner, piston, valve and seat identification. You can contact Pratt Oil with your parts request for review of the model, reference and component details.

Brand and model names identify the equipment described in the source material. Final parts matching depends on the complete pump and assembly information.
