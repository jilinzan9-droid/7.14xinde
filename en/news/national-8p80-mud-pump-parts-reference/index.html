Keep the power-end and fluid-end requests distinct

A maintenance list may combine a studded discharge module, liner bushing, piston rod and crosshead component. These parts belong to different assemblies and should be entered as separate RFQ lines. In the supplied 8P-80 component list, 1291280 identifies a studded discharge module, 1291165 a liner bushing and 1291277 a piston rod. Copy the full number as written, then add the component description and quantity.

The liner clamp and liner bushing are listed separately, and the intermediate rod wiper seal is distinct from the intermediate rod baffle. Those differences matter when a buyer compares a complete maintenance package with a quotation for one seal or one clamp. Crosshead requests also need the position or handedness taken from the matching drawing; a repeated generic description is not enough to confirm the correct part.
