PDF page numbers count from the first page of each reference file. Brand names identify the equipment or catalogue source. Send the nameplate, complete reference, quantity and drawing revision for a parts review.

For a Oilwell A-1400-PT mud pump parts enquiry, the complete component reference is more useful than the pump model alone. The Mud King Products catalogue separates expendable parts by nominal size, component family and installed arrangement. This guide transcribes the identifiable supplier-number entries associated with Oilwell A-1400-PT, with their original page and table context, so a buyer can assemble a clearer maintenance RFQ.

Model-Restricted Liner Adapter and L-Shaped Parts

The A-1400-PT liner sheet uses the MK-0284 series and mentions small-OD pattern MK-0283-XX. A pattern containing XX needs a completed supplier reference and dimensional confirmation; it should not be treated as a finished part number. The packing entries vary by liner size, so copy the size and packing together.

The conventional shared module table limits liner-adapter nut MK-10-300-182 and centering sleeve MK-10-300-186 to A1400-PT/A1700-PT, with an additional liner-size footnote for the sleeve. The separate L-shaped table identifies complete assembly MK-8488-1A for those models and distinguishes its large-OD retainer nut. These qualified entries are retained without asserting that neighbouring A850/A1100 components are equivalent.

Read the catalogue reference correctly

The accompanying reference list covers the identifiable number-bearing cells in the relevant source tables, not a complete original-manufacturer bill of materials. MK and MKG identify supplier catalogue references; their prefixes, punctuation and combined suffixes are retained. A value marked N/A or left blank is not replaced by an invented number. A shared model heading records the catalogue scope and does not by itself confirm interchangeability on an individual pump.

Information to send for parts matching

Send the pump nameplate and serial number, full reference including its prefix, required quantity and a photograph of the installed or removed component. For liners and pistons, add the nominal size and sealing arrangement. For fluid-end hardware, identify the module configuration and whether an individual component or a studded assembly is required. Keep any original equipment drawing number as a separate field rather than stripping the supplier prefix to assume an OEM match.

Review mud pump spare parts matching, compare liners, pistons, valves and seats, or send Pratt Oil a parts list for review. Matching and availability require confirmation; these references do not establish authorisation, inventory or a delivery promise.
