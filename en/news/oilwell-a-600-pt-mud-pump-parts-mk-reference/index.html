Oilwell A-600-PT Mud Pump Parts: Shared Expendables and A-600 Module References

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Identify Oilwell A-600-PT liners, valve-service components and supplier references with source-page context before preparing a parts enquiry.

For a Oilwell A-600-PT mud pump parts enquiry, the complete component reference is more useful than the pump model alone. The Mud King Products catalogue separates expendable parts by nominal size, component family and installed arrangement. This guide transcribes the identifiable supplier-number entries associated with Oilwell A-600-PT, with their original page and table context, so a buyer can assemble a clearer maintenance RFQ.

Shared Expendables and A-600 Module References

The expendables page is visibly headed A-350/600-PT, while the contents entry identifies A-600-PT. Its liner and rod references are retained with that joint-page context. For instance, the 5-inch liner is MK-0287-50, and the rod row distinguishes MK-0283-C, MK-0283-B and MK-0283-D as different components.

A separate module section explicitly names A-600-PT. It lists MK-10-304-052 for the studded discharge module, MK-10-304-054 for the valve guide and MK-10-305-097 for the valve cover. That module section is included for A-600, without extending it to A-350 merely because their expendables share a page. Compare the installed module, seal arrangement and required hardware before requesting an assembly.

Read the catalogue reference correctly

The accompanying reference list covers the identifiable number-bearing cells in the relevant source tables, not a complete original-manufacturer bill of materials. MK and MKG identify supplier catalogue references; their prefixes, punctuation and combined suffixes are retained. A value marked N/A or left blank is not replaced by an invented number. A shared model heading records the catalogue scope and does not by itself confirm interchangeability on an individual pump.

Information to send for parts matching

Send the pump nameplate and serial number, full reference including its prefix, required quantity and a photograph of the installed or removed component. For liners and pistons, add the nominal size and sealing arrangement. For fluid-end hardware, identify the module configuration and whether an individual component or a studded assembly is required. Keep any original equipment drawing number as a separate field rather than stripping the supplier prefix to assume an OEM match.

Review mud pump spare parts matching, compare liners, pistons, valves and seats, or send Pratt Oil a parts list for review. Matching and availability require confirmation; these references do not establish authorisation, inventory or a delivery promise.
