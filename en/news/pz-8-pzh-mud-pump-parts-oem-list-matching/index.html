Distinguish part numbers from figure positions

The manufacturer list instructs buyers to order by the part number and description; reference positions are provided to locate parts in the drawing. For example, “item 12” is insufficient because item numbers repeat in other assembly sections. Include the exact part number, assembly name and catalogue page.

Match the fluid-cylinder revision and interfaces

The selected fluid-end table is explicitly headed 5000 PSI and Wide Body in the source. It lists fluid-cylinder assembly 201PZL2039 and cylinder 205PZL029. The PZL characters within those listed numbers do not change the document’s stated PZH scope, and they do not justify applying an arbitrary PZL part to a PZH pump.

Check the installed cylinder, manifold interfaces, cover locks, valve guides, studs and seals. The source lists 300PZJ715 for the valve-cover gasket, while its suction-manifold-to-cylinder gasket is 25BE41. Similar-looking numbers from another supplier or earlier catalogue should be checked rather than substituted automatically.

Preserve the model and serial-number evidence

The ordering guidance requests the pump model and serial number. It also explains that right-hand and left-hand sides are viewed from the power end looking toward the fluid end. Include that orientation with photographs when requesting side-specific parts.
