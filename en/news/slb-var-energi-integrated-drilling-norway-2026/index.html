SLB Wins Vår Energi Offshore Drilling Services Scope

Published by Pratt Oil · 16 November 2026

SLB announced on 12 November 2026 that Vår Energi had selected it as the main integrated drilling services supplier for the Transocean Barents and Deepsea Bergen rigs on the Norwegian Continental Shelf. The initial scope runs for four years and includes four additional one-year extension options.

Two rigs and an integrated delivery model

The two harsh-environment semisubmersibles are to undertake exploration and development drilling. If the options are exercised, the scope could extend through 2034. The optional periods should therefore be distinguished from the initial four-year commitment.

SLB says the delivery model connects planning, digital workflows and field execution, working with Vår Energi and other service and technology providers. The selection adds drilling services to an existing relationship involving digital well and field planning and subsea production systems through SLB OneSubsea.

Source: SLB's 12 November 2026 announcement. The release does not name individual mud pump or drawworks spare-part references.

Procurement perspective: keep service scope and parts scope distinct

An integrated services announcement describes responsibilities at programme level. A replacement-parts request needs a different level of detail: the installed equipment, assembly drawing and individual purchase line. Buyers should establish that record before comparing offers for components used on their own rigs.

For drilling rig and drawworks parts, retain the full assembly designation, serial reference and drawing position. If the requirement concerns a complete assembly, name the parts expected with it. If it concerns individual wear items, list those items separately with their quantities.

For pump maintenance, a readable nameplate and the correct catalogue revision help connect the request to the intended model. The mud pump spare parts overview provides component and model entry points; it does not identify the pump configuration installed on either rig in this announcement.

When a purchase list contains both a drawing number and a manufacturer's marking, keep both fields. Record unclear characters as unresolved and attach photographs.
