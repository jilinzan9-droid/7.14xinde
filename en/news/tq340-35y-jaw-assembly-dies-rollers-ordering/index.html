TQ340-35Y Jaw Assemblies, Dies and Roller Orders

Published by Pratt Oil · 16 November 2026

For TQ340-35Y casing tong spares, state whether you need a complete jaw assembly or individual gripping and roller components. A quotation for teeth, a roller or a roller shaft has a different scope from a complete assembly. Keep the drawing number and component description together so the requested contents can be checked before an order is confirmed.

Use the drawing position to define the item

The TQ340-35Y parts and drawing-number reference includes several jaw assemblies. One listed assembly is 56.610.00. The same catalogue separately lists 24.100-03 as teeth and 56.610-03 as a roller.

These examples show why “jaw parts” is not a complete order description. Mark the component on the applicable drawing, retain its stated size and give a separate quantity. Do not assume that a jaw assembly reference includes every loose component shown elsewhere in the catalogue.

Keep both name-and-code pairs independent

The reference table places two component descriptions and two codes on many rows. The components on the left and right are separate entries. A line shared on the screen does not establish that the two parts form a kit or are supplied together.

When copying the table into a purchase list, place one component and its matching code on each order line. Keep the complete suffix, including letters at the end. Attach the original drawing page so a reviewer can see the surrounding assembly rather than work from an isolated number.

Specify assembly contents before comparing prices

For a complete jaw assembly request, list the components expected with it and the required pipe-size reference from the installed tong documentation. For an individual replacement, identify whether it is the die or teeth, roller, shaft, frame or another item. If a supplier proposes a different reference, ask for the difference to be stated in the quotation.

Related product categories include power tong dies and die plates and power tong rollers. These categories help route a request; the installed assembly drawing remains necessary to identify the actual replacement.

Prepare the TQ340-35Y inquiry

Send the complete nameplate designation, manufacturer, serial number and any additional model suffix. Add the marked assembly page, full drawing reference, component size and quantity required. Photographs should show the relevant part and its location in the assembly.

For several tong sizes, submit separate groups of order lines. Pratt Oil can review the stated references and requested supply scope with your inquiry. The quotation should identify the included components clearly so that a complete assembly offer is not compared directly with a price for individual wear parts.
