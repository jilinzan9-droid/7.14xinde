Identify power-end components from their own assembly

The crank-and-crosshead mechanism is identified as 66086.53.100 СБ on its assembly drawing. Its connecting rod, shaft, crosshead rod and crosshead have separate entries. The mechanism reference should not be used as though it were the part number of each internal component.

The URBO L1 catalogue uses other drawing families. No interchangeability between those references and the Bulanash entries is established by this guide. A complete enquiry should include the pump manufacturer, year, serial number and the source page used for identification.
