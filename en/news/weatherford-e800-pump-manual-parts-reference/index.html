Technical details and source references

Confirm the installed model, drawing revision and dimensions before ordering. Catalogue quantities do not indicate current stock.

Reference documents and scope

  • S1 — Weatherford E-800 parts catalogue / manual. Reference file: E-800.pdf.

PDF page numbers count from the first page of each reference file. Brand names identify the equipment or catalogue source. A listed number does not establish interchangeability, original-manufacturer authorisation, stock or a delivery commitment. Send the nameplate, complete reference, quantity and drawing revision for a parts review.

Identify the Weatherford E-800 correctly

The supplied E-800 operations and maintenance manual identifies the pump as a Weatherford single-acting triplex piston pump. It is a separate source identity from EMSCO F-800. Preserve that manufacturer and model distinction when preparing a parts request. The manual also explains that design options can vary from unit to unit. Its master parts lists may include systems not installed on a particular pump, so the equipment specification sheet remains important for confirming the actual configuration.

Read specifications as related limits, not one duty point

The introduction lists maximum rated power of 800 HP, maximum rated speed of 150 RPM, an 8.50-inch stroke and maximum discharge of 5000 psi. The same page gives separate output and pressure rows for different piston sizes. These values should not be combined into a promise that every bore achieves the maximum pressure and capacity simultaneously. They identify the manual configuration; they do not replace the operating documentation and verification needed for the installed equipment.

Separate assemblies from size-specific liner references

The master list identifies power-end assembly 1248524 and fluid-end assembly 1230800. The fluid-end section then lists manifold, gasket and studded-module references, while the liner section separates bore and material options. A request for E-800 liners therefore needs more than the model name. Preserve the exact liner number, bore and material stated in the relevant row. If an enquiry concerns a complete assembly, define the included components rather than assuming the master-list heading determines the quotation scope.

Prepare a matching enquiry

Send the Weatherford E-800 nameplate, serial number, equipment specification sheet and exact part number with quantity. For liners and pistons, include bore, material and existing markings. For an assembly, describe the required contents and attach the relevant manual page. Include photographs of the installed component and connections. Any proposed alternative should be identified separately and checked against the actual pump configuration before the quotation is accepted.

For a specific requirement, use mud pump spare parts enquiries. Request confirmation of the exact reference and configuration before accepting a quotation. A catalogue entry does not establish stock, delivery time, manufacturer authorization or suitability for a particular pump.
