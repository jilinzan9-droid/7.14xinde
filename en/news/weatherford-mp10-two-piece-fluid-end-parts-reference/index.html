Weatherford MP-10 Parts: Identifying the Two-Piece Fluid End

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Use the MP-10 two-piece fluid-end manual to distinguish suction and discharge modules, piston rods, liner retainers and seals.

The Weatherford MP-10 two-piece fluid end has separate suction and discharge components, with individual references for bare and studded modules. A purchasing list should identify this arrangement before comparing module or seal quotations. The supplied April 2013 manual 02034085 provides the reference structure used here; it should be matched to the installed pump and its serial-specific specification sheet.

Identify bare, studded and complete assemblies

The illustrated fluid-end assembly is 1924827 and its module assembly is 1316959. The module bill of material distinguishes bare suction module 1848450 from studded suction module 1317030, and bare discharge module 1848477 from studded discharge module 1317004. The words "module" and "fluid end" therefore need their full assembly description when included in an RFQ.

The same drawing separates the piston rod, studded sub rod, liner-retainer flange and liner seal. Its two-column parts table must be read within each column block: the piston rod is 1317518, the sub rod is 1317865 and the liner-retainer flange is 1917433. Liner material and gasket choices are listed in a separate table. A buyer should not substitute a seal number simply because the liner bore matches another MP-series pump.

What to send with the parts enquiry

For a matched request, provide the model and serial number, confirmation of the two-piece fluid end, the manual edition, item reference and quantity. Add the installed liner bore and material, photographs of module markings, and a list of components expected in each assembly. State whether replacement studs and nuts are required. Pricing and availability should be obtained for the current request rather than carried forward from a historical parts document.

For related purchasing information, see mud pump spare parts matching and liners, pistons, valves and seats. You can send your model and part references to Pratt Oil for component and document matching.
