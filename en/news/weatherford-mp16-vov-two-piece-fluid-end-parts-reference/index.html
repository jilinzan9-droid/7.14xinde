Weatherford MP-16 Parts: Valve-over-Valve and Two-Piece Fluid Ends

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Keep Weatherford MP-16 Valve-over-Valve and L-style references separate when matching modules, piston rods, wear plates and crankshafts.

Weatherford MP-16 spare parts should be identified by fluid-end version as well as the model name. The reference documents include a Valve-over-Valve manual and a separate two-piece, L-style manual. They share the MP-16 designation but list different module, rod and some power-end assembly references. Keeping those configurations and manual editions separate is essential when preparing a repair quotation.

Valve-over-Valve references

The January 2014 VoV manual identifies fluid-end assembly 1134282 and module assembly 2088480. It lists bare module 0889428 and studded module 1823019 separately. The VoV piston assembly uses piston rod 0889438. These references belong to the illustrated VoV arrangement; an L-style module should not be selected merely because it is also described as an MP-16 part.

Two-piece / L-style references

The January 2013 two-piece manual identifies fluid-end assembly 1877962, studded suction module 1317027 and discharge module 1316988. Its piston assembly uses two-piece rod 1317519, connecting stud 1323512 and sub rod 1317868. The drawings distinguish each component from the complete rod assembly, which helps define exactly what a supplier is being asked to quote.

What to send with the parts enquiry

The version distinction also reaches the power-end lists: the cited VoV manual lists crankshaft assembly 0908426, while the cited two-piece manual lists 0908406. These are separate source entries, not an approved replacement cross-reference. Keep their leading zeros and obtain a serial-specific match. Send the nameplate, installed fluid-end photographs, manual number and edition, original part number, item position, quantity and liner size with the enquiry.

Additional Russian manual editions: keep 7500 psi L-style references separate

The Russian legacy reference includes a two-piece L-style 7500 psi fluid end on PDF pages 119–123, with assembly 1830610, suction module 1317028 and discharge module 1317006. This list is separate from the English 5000 psi two-piece manual and its assembly 1877962. The other Russian translated 2013 manual covers the 5000 psi Valve-over-Valve configuration. Its crankshaft assembly is 0908426, whereas the earlier Russian edition lists 0908406. Match the source edition, pressure arrangement and installed nameplate before selecting a replacement. Use the original drawing to confirm liner and piston sizes; nominal dimensions and component references vary between editions.
