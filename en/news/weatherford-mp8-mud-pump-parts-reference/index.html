Weatherford MP-8 Parts: Fluid Modules, Liners and Seal References

Published by Pratt Oil · 20 October 2026

Match Weatherford MP-8 fluid-end modules, liner materials and seals using the April 2013 manual and original part references.

A Weatherford MP-8 spare-parts enquiry needs more than the pump model and a photograph. The supplied April 2013 manual separates the complete fluid end, module assembly, liners, gaskets and piston components. Its master list also covers design options that may not be fitted to every unit. The pump serial number and installed assembly should therefore be recorded before selecting a reference number.

These historical source values identify the illustrated configuration. Confirm the installed unit and its current equipment specification before selecting replacement parts.

Separate the fluid end from the individual module

The fluid-end assembly is listed as 2141749, while the module assembly is 2096928. Within that module, the catalog distinguishes a studded module, 1280240, from a bare module, 0889399. These descriptions define different purchasing scopes. A request for a module should state whether it includes studs, covers, valve guides and seals rather than assume every assembly quotation contains the same components.

Liner selection also needs the bore and material column. For the illustrated 4.000-inch size, the source lists chrome-iron liner 1317095 and zirconia-ceramic liner 1317107. The associated rubber and urethane gasket references are separate. The pressure chart varies with liner size, so the maximum discharge figure should not be applied to every liner option. Installed dimensions and the unit specification sheet remain part of the matching process.

What to send with the parts enquiry

Prepare separate RFQ lines for the module, liner, gasket and other components, keeping the original description with each number. Include the serial number, manual edition, drawing section, item position and required quantity. Photographs should show the removed part and its identifying marks. If the existing liner material is unknown, provide its dimensions and application details for review instead of selecting a material only from the catalog image.

For related purchasing information, see mud pump spare parts matching and liners, pistons, valves and seats. You can send your model and part references to Pratt Oil for component and document matching.
