ZQ203 Brake Band, Sliding Bushing and Spring Orders

Published by Pratt Oil · 16 November 2026

A ZQ203 power tong brake-parts request should identify each component rather than use a single description such as “brake kit.” The published parts reference lists the brake band, sliding bushing and spring separately. Use those individual references with the installed tong model, assembly drawing and required quantities when sending a parts list to Pratt Oil.

Three references to keep separate

The ZQ203 power tong parts reference contains these entries:

  • 01.06.01.00M — brake band, catalogue item 44.
  • 01.06-05M — sliding bushing, catalogue item 54.
  • 01.06-06M — spring, catalogue item 53.

These are three purchase items. If you require a set, state its contents explicitly and ask the quotation to identify each included component. A photograph of a complete mechanism can help show the location, but it should accompany the requested references rather than replace them.

Confirm which ZQ203 assembly is installed

The online reference contains 152 catalogue items and does not identify one exact ZQ203 variant. Send the complete nameplate designation, manufacturer and serial number where readable. Include the drawing title and revision used by your maintenance team.

The ZQ Series Drill Pipe Power Tongs page provides equipment-model references. A shared ZQ203 family name does not establish that every component in the list fits every variant. Where a marking differs from the catalogue, keep both versions visible and send a close photograph for review.

Separate catalogue quantity from order quantity

The quantity column describes the source assembly. Your purchase quantity may cover one tong, several machines or a spare-parts reserve. Add a separate “quantity required” column to your RFQ and specify whether each line is counted as individual pieces or a defined set.

For mixed fleets, group the lines by equipment identification. Do not total similar-looking components across machines until the responsible equipment team has confirmed the references. This keeps the supplier's quotation linked to the equipment that needs the parts.

Information to send for a quotation

Attach the complete tong nameplate, marked drawing position, full part number and purchase quantity for each line. Add photographs showing the component's location and any visible marking. If pins, fasteners or other pieces are also required, list their own drawing references rather than assuming they accompany the brake band.

Use part and drawing-number search to locate the published entries. This guide concerns purchasing information; assembly work and operating adjustments should follow the instructions for the identified tong.
