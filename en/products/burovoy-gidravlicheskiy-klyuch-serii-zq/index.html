Product Overview

Integrated Spinning and Torque Control

The open-throat head can disengage from the pipe string after the connection is made up or broken out. Combining spinning and final torque in one powered assembly reduces manual handling and helps limit thread damage when the correct jaws, rollers and operating settings are used.

Operating function

The hydraulic motor and transmission rotate the pipe connection, while the tong body applies controlled make-up or break-out torque. The backup arrangement reacts the torque during operation.

Typical service

Suitable for drill-pipe and tool-joint handling in land drilling, offshore drilling and workover service when the selected model and jaw/roller set match the connection outside diameter.

Model confirmation required

ZQ203 is a series designation, not a complete ordering code. Confirm ZQ203-100 versus ZQ203-125, standard versus Type II lifting configuration, manufacturer, nameplate, serial number, jaw/roller set and hydraulic/pneumatic supply before ordering.

ZQ Power Tong Buying Questions

Which ZQ model should I request?

Compare the required connection torque and tool-joint outside diameter with the model specifications. ZQ203-100 and ZQ203-125 have different maximum torque and rated pressure. Share your hydraulic supply details so the offered configuration can be checked.

Can I order ZQ203 spare parts separately?

Yes. Send the part or drawing number, quantity, full tong model and a photo. Use our ZQ203 parts reference to identify the component; a shared series name alone does not confirm interchangeability.

Are these drill pipe tongs the same as casing tongs?

The ZQ range on this page is for drill pipe and tool-joint handling. If your requirement is casing make-up or break-out, see our power tong selection page and provide the tubular size and connection torque.

What information is needed for price and delivery?

Send the model, quantity and destination. For replacement parts, include the manufacturer/nameplate and part number or photo. Price, available configuration and delivery time are confirmed in the quotation.
