Confirm the full pump model

F1600 may be written F-1600 on the nameplate or parts list. Include the manufacturer and the exact model marking. A similar model name does not establish component fit.

If your nameplate reads FB1600, use the separate FB1600 parts reference. For F1600HL or another suffix, send the complete marking and the corresponding parts drawing for review.

Prepare an F1600 parts quotation

  • Pump manufacturer, full model and clear nameplate photo.
  • Component name, part number and drawing position or revision.
  • Liner bore, connection dimensions, material or other documented requirements.
  • Quantity for each item and delivery country and city.

For projects in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Iraq, Oman, Kuwait, Qatar or Bahrain, include the delivery location and requested date so the quotation can address the project requirements.

Need help identifying an F1600 part?

Send a clear photo of the component, its installed position and any markings, together with the available dimensions or drawing. The F1600 parts identification and RFQ checklist explains how to organize missing or uncertain references.

For additional pump families, see mud pump spare parts supply and matching. Browse the BOMCO F-series component catalog for related parts.
