Service 01

Drilling Pump and Spare Parts

Drilling pumps are the working heart of oilfield circulation systems. Correct replacement parts help control pressure, cool the bit and reduce unplanned downtime.

We support BOMCO HH, RS, RG and QZ series, including F500, F800, F1000, F1300, F1600, F1600HL and F2200HL models.

  • Crankshaft and compensator assemblies
  • Pump bushings, pistons and valves
  • Rod extensions and crosshead components

Drilling Rig and Component Supply

A drilling rig depends on a coordinated supply of reliable hoisting, rotary and wellhead components. We help buyers identify the right replacement by model, drawing or product photo.

  • Crown blocks, hook systems and drilling winches
  • Rotary tables, swivels, top-drive and motor components
  • Hydraulic tongs, shale shakers and field accessories
  • Technical maintenance and on-site repair support for Chinese land rigs

Hydraulic Fracturing Solution

High-pressure fracturing pumps inject sand-bearing fluid into formations to improve flow from low-permeability reservoirs. Reliable fluid-end components are essential for continuous operation.

We support SPM TWS-600, TWS 2000/2250/2400/2500, QWS 1000/2500/2800 and TPB600, TPC600 and TPD600 pump applications.

  • Hydraulic housings and fluid-end components
  • Plungers, valves and sealing assemblies
  • Model matching from nameplate, drawing or product photo
