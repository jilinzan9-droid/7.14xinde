ORDERING DETAILS

Before you request
a quotation

Clear identification helps us quote the required scope.

What information should I send?

Send the full brake or PTO model, nameplate photo, part number, quantity and delivery country. For an unidentified component, add photos, dimensions and its position on the assembly drawing.

Do I need a friction disc or a complete disc assembly?

Specify whether the requirement is for friction material, a disc core, stationary plate or complete subassembly. A drawing or marked photo helps distinguish these items. See our disc and subassembly quotation guide.

Can one reference be used across Eaton and WPT brakes?

Confirm the installed equipment and drawing before selecting parts. The catalogue keeps the source families separate. Similar appearance or a shared size designation does not establish interchangeability.

Can I request a complete brake or PTO?

Yes. Include the full assembly designation and installation details, and state that your enquiry is for a complete unit. For Eaton WCB references, start with our brake model identification guide.
